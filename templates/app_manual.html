Core Platform Functionalities

Discover everything MACE-Connect offers to staff members and administration.

Roster Management

Centralized directory containing verified profiles of teaching, non-teaching, and contract staff. Access designations, blood group, contact numbers, and employment details instantly.

Departmental Categorization

Filtered views for all major departments including Computer Science (CSE), Electronics (ECE), Electrical (EEE), Mechanical (ME), Civil (CE), and Administrative staff.

Registration Approval Workflow

Seamless self-registration system for new staff. Requests are queued for administrator verification with single-click or bulk approval/rejection capabilities.

Realtime Growth & Analytics

Dynamic distribution charts and trend analysis tracking teaching vs. non-teaching ratios, departmental breakdown, and pending queue metrics.

Bulk Import & Data Processing

Administrators can upload batch staff records via Excel/CSV spreadsheets to automatically populate and sync database entries.

Native Mobile Experience

Optimized mobile UI/UX designed for rapid lookup on smartphones, offline caching support, and quick touch actions for phone numbers and email links.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding registration, access rights, and app usage.

How do I request a new staff account?

On the login screen, click "Sign up" to access the registration form. Fill in your full name, employee ID, department, email, mobile number, and designation. Once submitted, your request will be reviewed by the administrator.

What permissions do regular staff accounts have?

Regular staff accounts have read-only access to browse the roster, view departmental directories, search staff members, and download the mobile app. Administrative controls like approvals, data editing, and bulk imports are restricted to verified admins.

How do I reset my password if forgotten?

On the login screen, enter your registered email address and click "Forgot password?". A password reset link will be dispatched immediately to your email inbox.

Can I install the APK on iOS devices?

The .apk build is specifically for Android smartphones and tablets. iOS users can access the full feature set via Safari or Chrome on the web portal.
